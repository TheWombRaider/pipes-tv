# Reference analysis

Source: https://youtu.be/tCpnDTzRAjE (3d Pipes Screensaver 10 Hours, 854x480 stream, sampled by seeking and
by measuring pixel coverage in a canvas). "Observed" = seen directly in frames or measured; "Estimated" =
inferred from measurements.

| Area | Finding | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry | Thin tubes, ball joints about 1.5x the tube, ball caps on dead ends, rounded corners | Observed |
| Tube / lattice | Adjacent parallel tubes about 3 tube-diameters apart; cluster about 14 tubes across | Estimated from pixel spacing |
| Material | Satin shading, soft broad highlight, dark side still shows the colour | Observed |
| Light | Fixed to the camera, upper left in every scene | Observed (all scenes lit from the upper left) |
| Colours | Random per pipe: saturated and pale (yellow, teal, red, orange, grey, white, pale pink) | Observed |
| Camera | Static inside a scene; new random viewpoint and distance per scene; wide angle | Observed; about 65 degrees estimated |
| Population | 1 pipe at 4 s, 2 at 12 s, 3 at 19 s; 3 to 4 colours by the end | Observed in one scene |
| Growth | About 10 to 12 lattice units per second | Estimated from joint counts |
| Lifecycle | Scene 16 to 34 s (mean about 26 s), coverage 30 to 48 % of pixels at the end | Measured at 4 s steps over 5 minutes |
| Clear | Frozen picture, per-pixel random stipple to black over about 1.6 s, next scene starts at once | Observed in frames and a 10 Hz coverage trace |
| Teapot | Not seen in the sampled frames; the original rate is about 1 in 1000 joints | Not observed |
